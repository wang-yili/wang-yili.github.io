I am a theoretical physicist working on holography, quantum gravity, and strongly correlated systems.

My research interests are condensed matter theory and gravitational physics, as well as their interplay through
holographic dualities. On the gravity side, I work on black hole physics, AdS/CFT correspondence, and quantum
gravity. In the condensed matter context, I am particular interested in non-Fermi liquid behaviour, transport
in strange metals, and the Sachdev-Ye-Kitaev (SYK) model, as well as applications of holography to strongly
correlated systems.
